**Very early PlayStation alpha. Expect bugs, especially in Suikoden II.**

Some scenes still show the original character. In a reported Suikoden II seed, Gengen's first meeting showed Flik's portrait alongside Gengen's original sprite. Early story scenes and regular battles have been playtested. Later story battles, duels, and war battles still need testing.

The ZIP contains the seed generator for the original U.S. Suikoden I v1.1 and Suikoden II discs. It requires Python 3.11 or newer with Tk and your own disc image. Extract the ZIP and open `Seed-Randomizer.cmd` on Windows. Generate a seed, open its CUE in your emulator, and choose **New Game**.

Suikoden I offers expanded and guarded combat shuffles. Suikoden II shuffles 78 combat Stars and keeps Riou and all versions of Jowy in their original roles.
