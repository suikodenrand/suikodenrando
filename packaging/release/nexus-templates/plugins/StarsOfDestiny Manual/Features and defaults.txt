STARS OF DESTINY 0.14.6 - PLUGINS-ONLY FEATURES AND DEFAULTS

Included: combined randomizer 0.14.6, Suikoden Fix 1.5.2b (plugin
reports 1.5.2), optional dormant S1 ATB Preview 0.15.2 and default CFG files.
BepInEx 6 IL2CPP Windows x64 build 697 is required separately.
Launcher 1.0.7 is an OPTIONAL separate GitHub download, not part of this ZIP.

SUIKODEN I QUALITY OF LIFE
The four options start enabled. In the launcher, open Suikoden I > Quality of
Life to switch any option off or disable the group.
- Warehouse access adds Roc's Warehouse after Party Items once recruited.
- Shared battle items let each living active fighter use another living
  fighter's carried item. The charge comes from the owner's stack.
- Optimize equips one fighter from loose active-party gear and, after Roc
  joins, warehouse stock. Select either source or both in the launcher.
  Other fighters keep the gear they are wearing.
- The Viktor quick start matches starting companions to Tir's level with
  each companion's own stat growth.

S1 COUNT TIME BATTLE (OPTIONAL PREVIEW 0.5.2)
The battle plugin supports CTB and ATB. Both start off. With the component
installed, open Suikoden I > Battle & ATB, disable shuffled Unites and the
legacy Talisman preview, activate the component if needed, then enable the
battle preview and CTB. The optional launcher download also includes the CTB tuning editor.
Guide: https://github.com/suikodenrand/suikodenrando/blob/v0.14.6/docs/CTB-PROTOTYPE.md

BOTH GAMES
Seeded role Shuffle, five chosen companions or seeded random fixed roster;
optional protagonist shuffle and quick start; separate campaign mappings and
settings, shared F6 interface. Native story/recruitment conditions remain.
Names, portraits, party and battle appearance follow replacements. Supported
English dialogue mentions follow shuffled names; unsupported text/languages
and native custom-name/event tokens retain native handling. Both games have
shuffled Unite support using actual displayed fighters with native targeting.

JOINING WEAPONS / INVENTORY
Default inventory policy MatchRole: original role's full starting inventory,
including carried consumables and equipped state, with comparable wearable
equipment for replacements when needed. Donor retains replacement inventory.
Default weapon policy TirVariance (S1) / RiouVariance (S2): protagonist's
weapon level plus a seeded -3 to +3 offset, clamped to the legal range.
Alternatives MatchRole and Level1 are available. Inventory and weapon
policies are independent; weapon identity and rune setup remain replacement's.
First-join history/policies persist with campaigns; later earned equipment
is not intended to reset. Legacy saves without equipment metadata keep Donor;
upgrading does not retrofit inventory. All three roster modes are supported.

OPTIONAL / EXPERIMENTAL S1 FEATURES
Chest shuffle: deterministic campaign-seeded ordinary item rewards, with
potch and protected quest/recruitment rewards retained. Disabled by default.
ATB: native individual ready-hero menus/targeting, SPD meters, DEX influence,
queued commands and repeat actions. Bundled dormant and disabled; enable
manually as described in SETTINGS.txt, or use the optional launcher. S1
shuffled Unites and ATB/one-shot probes must not run together.

FRESH DEFAULTS
Seed=stars-of-destiny; Shuffle; normal openings; original role's starting
inventory; Tir/Riou weapon variance; dialogue replacement and shuffled Unites
on. Protagonist shuffle, S1 chest shuffle and ATB off. F6 starts Hidden and
visible modes use text only. Suikoden Fix gameplay multipliers are normal.
Existing configurations are not merged by manual installation; preserve them
on upgrade and deliberately choose settings for new campaigns.

NOT INCLUDED / LIMITS
No BepInEx loader/core/runtime, launcher/runtime, EXEs, install scripts,
nested archives, personal saves or generated interop assemblies in this ZIP.
Nuzlocke, S2 ATB/chest shuffle, and extra rune slots are not included. Wider S1/S2 story, recruit and Unite playtesting is
still needed. An earlier native equipment check observed Meg replacing Viktor,
first-join gear and a save; reload was not observed. This packaging split
does not change gameplay binaries or expand that native test coverage.
